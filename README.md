# Spaceship Adventure

A third-person space shooter built with **Unity3D** and **C#**

## About the Game

**Spaceship Adventure** is a third-person space shooter set in a sci-fi future. Piloting a spaceship, the player fights through fields of randomly generated asteroids, destroying enemies to earn score and in-game currency. That currency unlocks new ships and weapons. Once everything is unlocked, the goal shifts to beating your own high score.

## Gameplay

- The player controls a spaceship from a third-person perspective.
- Destroying enemies earns **score** and **money**, used to unlock new ships and weapons.
- The ship has limited **energy**, consumed by firing weapons — encouraging players to manage it carefully and refill it by defeating enemies.
- The ship also has **health**, which depletes when hit by enemy fire and can be restored the same way as energy. If health reaches zero, the run ends and restarts.
- Both energy and health capacity depend on the selected ship, and energy consumption depends on the selected weapon.
- Combat takes place among randomly generated asteroid fields populated by two enemy types:
  - **Turrets** — stationary, mounted on asteroids.
  - **Enemy ships** — mobile, actively pursue and attack the player.
- Enemies engage and give chase once the player enters their line of sight.

## Controls

- **Keyboard** — directional flight and roll (fully rebindable in the settings menu).
- **Mouse** — pitch and yaw.
